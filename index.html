I study how people perform, adapt, and make decisions in complex healthcare systems. My current work focuses on healthcare human–robot interaction, physiological sensing, and AI-assisted assessment of human performance.

My research draws on both behavioral evidence and physiological signals. I have worked with multimodal data including EEG, ECG, and subjective measures, as well as econometric models of healthcare choice and quantitative models for pandemic preparedness.

Outside the lab: swimming, long walks, reading, visiting art museums, and a good Americano — plus the occasional trip to the jungle in League of Legends. Emerald user on the Korean server.
